Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Spoider Finds ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Basic analytics data such as pages visited, time on site, clicks on product links, and referral source (e.g. Pinterest).
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and profile photo, as provided by your Google account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Information you add to your profile, such as a username and the products you choose to show as owned.
  • Information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address if you contact us.
  • Cookies used by third-party services (like Amazon Associates) to track affiliate referrals.

How We Use Information

  • To create and run your account and profile.
  • To understand which content and products our audience finds useful.
  • To respond to messages sent through our contact page.
  • To improve site performance, security, and user experience.

We do not sell your personal information.

Google Sign-In

When you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares only your basic profile (name, email address, and profile photo) with us. We use it solely to create and secure your Spoider Finds account and display your profile. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Public Profiles

Profiles on Spoider Finds are public. Your display name, profile photo, username, and the products you mark as owned may be visible to other visitors. Please do not add information you do not want to be public.

Service Providers

We use Supabase (authentication and database), Google (sign-in and analytics), and GitHub Pages (hosting). These providers process data on our behalf.

Third-Party Links

Our site contains affiliate links to Amazon and potentially other retailers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. Please review their respective privacy policies.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies or local storage to keep you signed in, to improve your browsing experience, and to allow affiliate partners to track referred purchases. You can clear or disable them through your browser settings at any time, but you may be signed out.

Your Rights

You may request details of any personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete your account and data, by contacting us at hello@spoiderfinds.com.

Children

Spoider Finds is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
